Bridging Music Information Retrieval and computational bioacoustics.

01

About the workshop

MuPAS is a workshop that brings together researchers in Music Information Retrieval and computational bioacoustics to discuss the intersections of the two fields.

The study of animal sounds has always drawn on many disciplines, from acoustics and field recording to linguistics, musicology and machine learning. Yet its computational tools have come mostly from speech science and general signal processing, while music research has stayed at the edge of the conversation.

We think music has more to offer. Like bioacoustics, MIR works with scarce labels and highly variable sound, and it has mature tools for melody, rhythm and structure. The workshop takes stock of what music processing has already contributed to the field, and aims to build a lasting community between the two.

02

Where music meets animal communication

  • Shared problemsScarce annotations, weak supervision, ambiguous segmentation and highly variable recording conditions.
  • Variation like music'sDifferences across species can resemble differences across instruments and genres more than across human languages.
  • Tools MIR already hasMelodic contour analysis, beat and meter tracking, source separation and self-supervised music representations.
  • The question of songA musical lens on animal communication speaks to biomusicology, musicality across species and the origins of music.
  • Shared dataDatasets that cross both communities, giving out-of-domain benchmarks and common evaluation practices.
